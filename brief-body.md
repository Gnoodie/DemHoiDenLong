## Direction contract

THESIS: A multiplane parallax environment that replaces static menus with a living, breathing festival space where the chosen Lân physically floats in mid-air.
OWN-WORLD: Night sky indigo background layered with soft mist, glowing lantern particles, and 2D vector Lân sprites. UI elements are flat and utilize glowing gold and jade accents.
STORY: The visitor enters the festival night, feels the magical energy of the Lân, checks their collected star currency, and immediately hits the glowing Play button to continue the dance.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Center stage is dominated by the glowing Lân, floating softly. At the top edge, currency (stars) and settings. At the bottom, a large, rounded Jade Green 'Play' button. Swiping left/right shifts the Lân and dynamically changes the ambient lighting layer (e.g., warmer for Fire, cooler for Water).
FORM: Multiplane Cel-Animation Dawn (Index 5), seed key 142fa913.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
